HOW TO OPERATE

MOVEMENT

Tap the ground to move the selected soldier. Tap a soldier or portrait to select. Use ALL to move the squad in formation.

Drag empty ground to pan. Pinch to zoom. Drag the minimap to reposition the camera.

CROUCH slows movement and halves your visibility, especially inside bushes.

STEALTH

Guards have amber vision cones. Avoid them, and avoid the sweeping searchlights. A guard who fills his suspicion meter raises the alarm.

Approach an unaware guard from behind and use KNIFE for a silent kill. Bodies do not raise alarms.

COMBAT

Tap a guard to fire. Gunfire is loud and alerts every soldier nearby. The sniper has long range and lethal accuracy, but a slow rifle.

The sapper carries 3 grenades. Arm one, then tap the target ground. Frags wound everyone in the blast.

OBJECTIVES

Take the battle plans from the command bunker. Plant a satchel on the radio tower with the sapper. Then bring every living soldier to the green smoke on the north ridge.

Free the prisoner in the side pen for a bonus. If every soldier falls, the mission is lost.
